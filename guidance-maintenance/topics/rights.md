# Rights, notices and public output

Applies when adding or adapting material not originally authored by the owner,
changing licenses or notices, or preparing guidance for publication. Depends on
the [entry](../ENTRY.md) rules and [authoring](authoring.md).

## Original and third-party material

- Owner-controlled original material may be adapted under the bundle's license
  with owner permission. Record privately the file/section, author or control,
  adaptation and decision.
- Third-party material: link to official or upstream sources. Do not import
  tutorials, skill bodies, code, configurations, lint sources, datasets, icons or
  scripts. A local note may say when to consult a source and what local rule
  prevails; it does not copy the source.
- Unknown provenance or license: hold the material. Do not assume a permissive
  license or infer an upstream from a similar name. Request an owner decision when
  the hold blocks preserving a rule.
- Reuse only audited primitives whose license, provenance and notices are recorded.

## Notices

- Each bundle declares a complete license text and a notice describing provenance,
  revision, exclusions and that the license does not relicense third parties.
- Product and tool names do not imply endorsement or grant trademark rights.
  License appendix placeholders are not a project attribution.

## Public output review

- Before requesting publication, check public output for private paths, product
  APIs, product architecture rules, consumer logs, classification or source-report
  dumps, internal revision pins and secrets.
- Product-specific or uncertain content belongs in the private product guidance.
- Hashes prove integrity, not trust or approval. Repository creation, publication,
  release and consumer activation each follow the repository's own authorization.
